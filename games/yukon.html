Goal

The Goal

Yukon is a somewhat similar to Klondike but with no stock pile. It is kind of a mix between Klondike and Thornq/Scorpion. The goal is to move all the cards to the four stacks in the upper right of the window.

Play

How to Play

Build sequences down by alternating colors (red on black, black on red). Groups of cards can be moved; the cards below the one to be moved do not need to be in any order, except that the starting and target cards must be built in sequence and in alternate color. For example, a group starting with a Red 3 can be moved on top a Black 4, and the cards below the Red 3 can differ. You may move a king into any empty stack.

You may place one card at a time onto the output stacks in the upper right. Aces may go onto any empty output stack. Other cards must go onto the same suit and in ascending order (ace, 2, etc, king). Note that the computer does this for you automatically.

Mouse

Using the Mouse

The left button is used to move cards around. Move the mouse over the card, press and hold the left button, and move the mouse. The cards follow the mouse.

If you click the left mouse button twice quickly without moving the mouse (double clicking), the computer will Attempt to do "the right thing" - if you double click on the bottom card, it tries to find the card that goes on it, else it tries to move the card you double clicked on to the bottom of another stack.

The right button can be used to "peek" at an obscured card.

If your mouse has buttons 4 and 5, the forward button will restart the game and the back button will undo the last move.

Keys

Using the Keyboard

The a key toggles the hint arrows on/off.

The q and Esc keys quit the game. The F2 and r keys restart the game. The F1 and h keys show this help. The backspace key will undo the last move.

When viewing help, the space bar, F1 or Esc return you to your game.
