Goal

The Goal

The goal of solitaire (Klondike) is to move all the cards to the four stacks in the upper right of the window.

My personal goal is to make money and have fun! :-)

Play

How to Play

You may flip one card at a time off the deck (upper left, face down) onto the hole next to it (face up). When the deck is empty, click on that spot again and the hole's cards are moved back to the deck, and you start flipping again.

In the lower part of the window, you may move any card onto another card if the card you move is a different color and the next lower value (ace is one, king is 13). You may move stacks of cards in this sequence onto other cards to grow these stacks.

You may place one card at a time onto the output stacks in the upper right. Aces may go onto any empty output stack. Other cards must go onto the same suit and in ascending order (ace, 2, etc, king).

In vegas style, you pay $52 to play and you win $10 for each card that you place in the outdeck. The deck is only turned 3 times. 3 cards are flipped from the deck at a time. Undo is disabled. At the bottom left of the table you will see your current earnings, how many deck turns are left in this hand, as well as how many hands have been dealt.

Mouse

Using the Mouse

The left button is used to move cards around. Move the mouse over the card, press and hold the left button, and move the mouse. The cards follow the mouse. Note that the number of cards you carry automatically varies as you move it over the various stacks; this indicates how many cards you may drop on that stack.

If you click the left mouse button twice quickly without moving the mouse (double clicking), the computer will move what it can from what you clicked on to wherever it can go, if there is an obvious choice.

The right button can be used to "peek" at an obscured card.

If your mouse has buttons 4 and 5, the forward button will restart the game and the back button will undo the last move.

Keys

Using the Keyboard

The q and Esc keys quit the game. The F2 and r keys restart the game. The F1 key shows this help. The backspace key will undo the last move.

The v key toggles Vegas mode on/off.

The 3 key switches to 3 card draws from the deck. The 1 key switches back to the default 1 card draw from the deck.

When viewing help, the space bar, F1 or Esc return you to your game.
