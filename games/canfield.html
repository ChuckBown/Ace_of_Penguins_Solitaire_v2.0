Goal

The Goal

The goal of Canfield is to move all the cards to the four stacks in the upper right of the window.

My personal goal is to make money and have fun! :-)

Play

How to Play

Each shuffle randomly picks

Setup:

Reserve: 13 cards face down at the upper left of the window.

Foundation: The base rank is randomly chosen on each shuffle and is the first card added to the empty spaces at the top right of the window. The other 3 cards of that same rank are added to the foundations as you find them.

Stock: Your draw pile is at the bottom left.

Tableau: Deal 4 cards face up in a row below the foundation cards.

How to Play:

Foundations: Build up by suit, wrapping from King to Ace if needed (e.g., Ace can go on a King).

Tableau: Build down in alternating colors (red on black, black on red). You can move single cards or packed groups.

Empty Spaces: If an empty spot opens up in the tableau, it must be filled immediately by the top card of your reserve pile.

Stock Draw: Flip cards from the stock (traditionally 3 at a time, or 1 at a time for an easier game) into a waste pile, and play the top waste card onto the tableau or foundations. You can cycle through the stock pile as many times as you like.

Mouse

Using the Mouse

The left button is used to move cards around. Move the mouse over the card, press and hold the left button, and move the mouse. The cards follow the mouse. Note that the number of cards you carry automatically varies as you move it over the various stacks; this indicates how many cards you may drop on that stack.

If your mouse has buttons 4 and 5, the forward button will restart the game and the back button will undo the last move.

Keys

Using the Keyboard

The q and Esc keys quit the game. The F2 and r keys restart the game. The F1 key shows this help. The backspace key will undo the last move.

The 1 key will switch to 1 card draws from the Stock. The 3 key switches back to the default 3 card draws from the Stock.

When viewing help, the space bar, F1 or Esc return you to your game.
